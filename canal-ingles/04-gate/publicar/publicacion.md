Why your gate sags (and which way the brace goes)
```

**Descripción:**
```
A gate frame is a rectangle that wants to turn into a parallelogram. The gate's weight pulls down on the latch corner until it drags. One diagonal brace, running from the bottom on the hinge side up to the top on the latch side, works in compression and keeps the gate square.

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Building codes and gate hardware vary by location: check your local code and a licensed professional before you build.
