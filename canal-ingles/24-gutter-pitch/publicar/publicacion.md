Flo hung her gutters perfectly level. Level gutters hold water, so they fill up and spill over the front onto the flower beds and the foundation. A common target is about 1/4 inch of drop every 10 feet, always toward the downspout; snap a string line and hang the brackets on it. Still overflowing? You may need more downspouts, often one every 30 to 40 feet.
