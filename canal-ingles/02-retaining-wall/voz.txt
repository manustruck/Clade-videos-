Your retaining wall is going to tip! Here's why. You stacked the blocks, filled behind them with plain dirt, and called it a day. Looks great, right? Then it rains. Water soaks the dirt and has nowhere to go. It piles up behind the wall, and pushes harder and harder. A few storms later… the wall leans. Whoops! Here's the fix. First, a foot of clean gravel behind the wall. Next, a perforated pipe at the bottom, wrapped in fabric, sloping to an outlet. Now the water drains out, and the wall stays put. Same blocks. Same dirt. Way less drama!
