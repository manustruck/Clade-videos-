Water that can't escape piles up behind a retaining wall and pushes harder with every storm. A foot of clean gravel behind the wall, a perforated pipe wrapped in fabric at the bottom, and an outlet for the water are the common fix.

For general education only. Building codes vary by location, and tall walls or poor soil need an engineer — check your local code and a licensed professional before you build.
