## YouTube Shorts

**Título:**
```
Is treated wood safe for raised garden beds? (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo says treated wood will poison your tomatoes. That was true of the old arsenic-based wood (CCA), which was pulled from residential use at the end of 2003. Today's treated lumber uses copper. In an Oregon State University study of raised beds built with copper azole lumber, extra copper stayed within about 1 inch of the boards and didn't build up in the vegetables. Never reuse old pre-2004 boards, and plant a few inches in from the wood.

Team Norm or Team Flo? Tell us in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Check the lumber tag and your local Extension office.

#shorts #raisedbeds #gardening #treatedwood
```
