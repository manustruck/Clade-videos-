**Título:**
```
Can river rocks explode in a fire pit? (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo says exploding rocks are a myth. Norm says river rocks can hold water deep inside. Heat a wet, porous rock fast and that water turns to steam with nowhere to go, so the rock can crack or pop. Use fire brick or dense stone made for fire pits inside the ring, and keep river rock away from the flames.

Team Myth or Team Danger? Tell us in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Follow your fire pit maker's instructions and local fire rules.
```
