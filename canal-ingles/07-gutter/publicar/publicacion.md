## YouTube Shorts

**Título:**
```
Downspout too short? It's flooding your basement
```

**Descripción:**
```
A downspout that dumps water right at the wall saturates the soil next to your foundation. That water pushes on the wall and finds any tiny crack. Add an extension that carries the water 6 to 10 feet away, and slope the ground away from the house (about 6 inches over the first 10 feet).

Does your downspout dump at the wall? Tell Norm in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Local rules, soil and house design vary: check your local code and a licensed professional.

#shorts #gutters #downspout #basement
```
