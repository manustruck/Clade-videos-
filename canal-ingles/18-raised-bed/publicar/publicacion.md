Raised bed bowing? Thicker boards won't fix it (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo filled her tall raised bed to the top. Wet soil is heavy and pushes out on every wall, and the middle of the long side bows first. The fix: a cross tie (a board or a threaded rod with wide washers) from one long side to the other, two ties for beds longer than about 8 feet, and strong corner posts that are screwed, not nailed, and sunk into the ground.

Team Thick Boards or Team Cross Tie? Tell us in the comments!
