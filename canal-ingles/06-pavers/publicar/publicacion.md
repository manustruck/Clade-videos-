Your patio will wave (and it's not the pavers' fault)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Pavers laid straight on dirt sink as the soil softens with rain and frost, and the patio turns into waves. The fix is three layers: 4 to 6 inches of compacted crushed stone (in thin lifts), about 1 inch of smooth bedding sand, then the pavers locked in with edge restraint spiked into the base.

Did you skip the base? Tell Norm in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Soil, climate and codes vary: check your local code and a licensed professional before you build.
