## YouTube Shorts

**Título:**
```
How far should a fire pit be from the house? (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo put her fire pit right by the door. Sparks drift and can land on siding, decks and tree branches, and heat rises into anything overhead. Most safety guides say at least 10 feet from the house and anything that burns; some towns require 25. Never under branches or a covered porch, keep a hose or extinguisher close, and never leave the fire alone.

Team Cozy or Team 10 Feet? Tell us in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Always check your local fire rules.

#shorts #firepit #firesafety #backyard
```
