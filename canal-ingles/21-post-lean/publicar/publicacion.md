**Descripción:**
```
Flo set her fence post in a hole one foot deep. The wind pushes the fence like a sail, and where the ground freezes, frost heave lifts the post a little more every winter. A common rule: bury about one third of the post (an 8 ft post goes almost 3 ft deep), go below your local frost line in cold climates, and tamp the gravel or soil every few inches.

Team 1 Foot or Team 1/3 Deep? Tell us in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Check your local frost depth with your building department.

#shorts #fencepost #fence #diy
```
