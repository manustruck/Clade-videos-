## YouTube Shorts

**Título:**
```
French drain not draining? Check the slope (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo dug her drain with no level, and the pipe has a dip in the middle. Water only flows downhill, so it sits in the low spot, dirt settles in it, and the drain clogs. The fix: a steady fall the whole way with no dips, at least 1/8 inch per foot (about an inch every 8 feet), checked with a string line or level, and an outlet that daylights at the lowest point of the yard.

Team Just Dig It or Team Slope It? Tell us in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Check local rules before draining toward a neighbor or street.

#shorts #frenchdrain #drainage #diy
```
