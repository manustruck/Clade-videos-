```
Flat patio = wet basement? Slope it away (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo made her patio perfectly flat. But flat means rain has nowhere to go: it puddles by the door or runs back to the wall and into the basement. Slope the patio away from the house, about 1/4 inch per foot (a 10 ft patio drops about 2½ inches). On a 4 ft level, the low end sits about an inch lower. You'll never feel it, but the rain will.

Team Flat or Team Sloped? Tell us in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Check your local code; some surfaces and climates call for a different slope.

#shorts #patio #pavers #diy
```
