## YouTube Shorts

**Título:**
```
Is your treehouse killing the tree? (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo built her treehouse with 200 nails and a rope around the trunk. Every nail is a wound, and lots of small holes mean lots of dead wood. A rope doesn't grow with the tree, so it strangles the bark. Builders say one big fastener beats a dozen small ones: a few large treehouse attachment bolts, nothing wrapped around the trunk, and room for the tree to grow and sway.

Team Nails or Team Big Bolts? Tell us in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Ask a certified arborist before attaching anything to a tree.

#shorts #treehouse #trees #diy
```
