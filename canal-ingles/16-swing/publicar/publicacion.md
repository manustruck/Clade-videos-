## YouTube Shorts

**Título:**
```
Porch swing on screw hooks? Don't (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo hung her porch swing with two screw hooks into the ceiling boards. Ceiling boards aren't structural, and a screw hook only grips with its threads, so every swing pulls it looser. The safer way: an eye bolt that goes all the way through the joist, with a washer and nut on top, plus closed hooks or quick links.

Team Hooks or Team Bolts? Tell us in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Check your swing's load rating and a licensed professional if unsure.

#shorts #porchswing #diy #homeimprovement
```
