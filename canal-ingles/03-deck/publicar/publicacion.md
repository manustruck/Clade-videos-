```
Deck ledger nailed to the house? Here's the danger
```

**Descripción:**
```
Nailing a ledger board to the house isn't enough. Rain sneaks behind the board, the wood rots, and every step pulls on the nails until the deck lets go. Flashing on top and 1/2-inch bolts through the board into the house frame are the common fix.

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Building codes vary by location, and deck connections are safety-critical: check your local code and a licensed professional before you build.
```
