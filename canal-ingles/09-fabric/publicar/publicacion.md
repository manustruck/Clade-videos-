**Título:**

Is landscape fabric a scam? (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo says landscape fabric is a total scam. She's half right: in flower beds, mulch breaks down into soil right on top of the fabric, seeds sprout in it, and roots grow through. Under a gravel path, though, fabric keeps the stones from sinking into the mud. Verdict: skip it in beds (use thick mulch), keep it under gravel.

Team Fabric or Team No Fabric? Fight in the comments!
