Flo's pergola has four big posts and beams on top, but push it sideways and the square turns into a diamond. That's racking. The fix is triangles: 45 degree knee braces where every post meets the beam, in both directions, and posts anchored solid (deep footings or bolted bases).

Team Big Posts or Team Braces? Tell us in the comments!
