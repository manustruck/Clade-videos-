```
Concrete control joints: just for looks? (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo poured a smooth slab with no lines. But concrete shrinks as it dries, and without joints it cracks wherever it wants, often right across the middle. Control joints are a weak spot on purpose, so the crack hides inside the line. Common rule: joint spacing in feet about 2 to 3 times the slab thickness in inches (8 to 12 ft for a 4 in slab), cut about 1/4 of the thickness deep, and cut early. Pour on a compacted gravel base.
```
