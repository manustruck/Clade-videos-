**Título:**

Your shed is sinking (here's why)
```

**Descripción:**
```
A shed set straight on the grass sits on soft soil. Rain washes the soil out from under one corner, the shed leans, the door jams and the floor rots. Dig out the grass, add 4 to 6 inches of compacted crushed stone (about a foot wider than the shed on each side), and make it level.

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Local rules, soil and shed size vary: check your local code and a licensed professional before you build.

#shorts #diy #shed #backyard #landscaping #homeimprovement
