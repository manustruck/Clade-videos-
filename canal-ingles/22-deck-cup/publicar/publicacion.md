Deck boards cupping? Bark side isn't the fix (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo swears bark side up stops cupping. It helps a little, but the bigger cause is moisture: a low deck sits over damp ground, the underside stays wet while the sun dries the top, so the board cups. Experts point to underside airflow (open, vented sides) and gravel over a ground cover to keep the ground from wetting the boards.

Team Bark Up or Team Airflow? Tell us in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!
