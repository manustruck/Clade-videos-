Your fence post is going to rot! And guess who did it? You did! You dug a hole, dropped in the post, and filled it with concrete. All the way around. Even underneath! Looks solid, right? Nope. You just built a cup! Rain runs down the post and has nowhere to go. It sits right here, at the ground line. Wet wood plus air equals rot. A few years later… one little push. Oops! Here's the fix. First, six inches of gravel, so water drains out the bottom. Next, pour the concrete around the post, never under it. Then slope the top, so rain runs away. Same post. Same concrete. Twice the life!
