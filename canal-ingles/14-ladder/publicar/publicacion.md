Ladder angle: the 4 to 1 rule (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo says a flatter ladder is safer. Too flat and the feet kick out; too steep and it tips backward. The 4 to 1 rule (OSHA): for every 4 feet of height to where the ladder rests, set the feet 1 foot out. 10 feet to the gutter = 2.5 feet out. Quick check: toes at the ladder feet, arms straight out, hands on a rung. Climbing onto a roof? Let the ladder stick up 3 feet past the edge.

Team Flatter or Team 4 to 1? Tell us in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Follow your ladder's labels and safety instructions.

#shorts #ladder #laddersafety #diy
