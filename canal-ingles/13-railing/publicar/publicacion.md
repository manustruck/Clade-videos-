**Título:**

Deck railing post screwed to the rim? (Flo vs Norm)
```

**Descripción:**
```
Flo says her deck railing is solid: the post is screwed right into the deck. But it's screwed into one board, the outside rim. Push on the top and the post works like a crowbar. US code (IRC) asks guards to resist 200 lb pushed on the top, and in Virginia Tech tests some posts held only by screws let go at about a quarter of that. The fix ties the post into the deck joists: blocking, through bolts and a metal hold-down.

Team Safe or Team Fall? Tell us in the comments!

Follow for more backyard builds that won't fail!

For general education only. Guards are safety-critical: check your local code and a licensed professional.
